My Academic Project

Nike: Move Beyond Limits

As part of The Consumer Journey module, our group developed a consumer-centred omnichannel marketing campaign for Nike.

The campaign focused on beginner runners aged 18–30 and explored how Nike could engage consumers through digital platforms, physical stores and running communities.

The project applied the SOSTAC framework, consumer journey analysis and consumer behaviour theory to develop a structured marketing campaign.

Skills Development and Learning

Throughout The Consumer Journey module, I developed important employability skills, including teamwork, communication, time management, critical thinking and problem-solving. Working on the Nike: Move Beyond Limits marketing campaign helped me understand how these skills are used in real business situations. I learnt how consumer behaviour influences marketing decisions and how businesses develop strategies to attract customers.

Teamwork and Communication

Working on the Nike campaign helped me understand the importance of teamwork and effective communication. I learnt that sharing ideas, listening to different opinions and respecting others can improve group performance. Group discussions also helped me develop confidence in expressing my thoughts. However, I realised that communicating different ideas clearly can sometimes be challenging. I need to improve my confidence when speaking and presenting information.

Time Management and Problem-Solving

The project helped me understand the importance of managing workloads and meeting deadlines. I learnt how planning tasks and setting priorities can support successful project completion. Understanding the SOSTAC framework and analysing Nike's target audience also helped me develop analytical and problem-solving skills. However, I sometimes found it difficult to organise information and connect marketing theories with practical examples. This showed me that I need to improve my research and planning skills.

Career Awareness and Future Development

The module increased my understanding of career opportunities in marketing and business management. I learnt that employers value communication, teamwork, digital knowledge and the ability to understand customer needs. The Nike project also helped me recognise the importance of ethical marketing and responsible decision-making.

In the future, I want to improve my presentation skills, develop stronger digital marketing knowledge and gain practical work experience. I also plan to participate in more group activities and professional networking opportunities. Overall, this module helped me recognise my strengths, identify areas for improvement and prepare for future employment in the business industry.

My Future Career Goals

My goal is to continue developing the communication, teamwork, analytical and digital skills required in the business and marketing industry.

I aim to gain practical experience, strengthen my professional confidence and apply my knowledge of consumer behaviour in future employment.
