My Academic Project

Nike: Move Beyond Limits

As part of The Consumer Journey module, our group developed a consumer-centred omnichannel marketing campaign for Nike.

The campaign focused on beginner runners aged 18–30 and explored how Nike could engage consumers through digital platforms, physical stores and running communities.

The project applied the SOSTAC framework, consumer journey analysis and consumer behaviour theory to develop a structured marketing campaign.

Skills Development and Learning

During the Consumer Journey process, I have gained some of the valuable employability skills such as teamwork, time management, communication, critical thinking and problem solving. Within the Nike: Move Beyond Limits marketing campaign I have applied and discovered how such skills can be applied to business. I have understood the impact of consumer behaviour on marketing and how companies target consumers.

Teamwork and Communication

I have learned the value of communication and teamwork whilst working on the Nike campaign. I have also learned that listening to others, respecting others and sharing opinions can lead to a more successful team. I have also gained confidence in communicating my opinions through group discussion. I also found that explaining various concepts can sometimes be difficult. I would like to work on my confidence whilst talking and presenting.

Time Management and Problem-Solving

I have gained an insight into meeting deadlines and managing workload. I have also learned how project success can be achieved through planning and prioritisation. I have also developed my problem solving and analytical skills through studying Nike's customer segments and learning about the SOSTAC model. I also found that I had trouble linking marketing concepts to real life situations and organising information. I have realised that I should work on my plan and research skills.

Career Awareness and Future Development

This module has given me more insight into careers in management and business. I have realised that employers are looking for skills in customer understanding, digital skills, teamwork, communication and knowledge. I also gained an awareness of ethical decision making and ethical marketing from the Nike project.

I would like to enhance my presentation skills and gain work experience by gaining a stronger understanding of digital marketing. I also intend to increase my participation in professional networking and group events. In general the module has enabled me to gain an understanding of my abilities, areas that can be enhanced and to also gain an insight into future work within the business environment.

My Future Career Goals

My goal is to continue developing the communication, teamwork, analytical and digital skills required in the business and marketing industry.

I aim to gain practical experience, strengthen my professional confidence and apply my knowledge of consumer behaviour in future employment.
